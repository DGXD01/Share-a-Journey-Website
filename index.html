Six steps

How It Works

The same rules the buttons on this page actually follow.

  1. Post a journey. Drivers offer a ride, passengers ask for one. Each post gets its own record ID and management code.
  2. Fill your details once. Your name, phone and email are saved the first time and reused for every later post or application.
  3. Browse and select. Filter by origin, destination, date and post type. You can select several journeys of the same type and apply in one batch.
  4. Every application is its own record. In a batch, each journey still gets a separate record ID, management code and status.
  5. The publisher decides. An application stays Pending and the journey stays public until the publisher accepts one applicant.
  6. Manage with your code. Use the record ID and code to reopen a record, and use My Activity to follow your own posts and applications.

Who sees your contact details

  • A journey card and its public details show the publisher's phone and email.
  • An applicant's details are visible only in the publisher's Received Applications view.
  • Management codes never appear on a public card or in public details.

How this page stores your data

  • Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Another browser or another computer starts empty.
  • Press Refresh Status (or reload the page) to re-read the local snapshot. There is no server pushing updates.
  • Two people cannot be online on the same device at the same time; switch identities to act as each traveller in turn.
